What it does

Choose the ayahs, hear them, and make a clip.

Any ayahs you choose

Pick a surah, tap the first ayah and the last. Or start from a favourite like Ayat al-Kursi or Al-Ikhlas.

A whole ayah, or part of one

Trim the recitation to exactly the words you want to share. The Arabic is never changed.

Easy edit

Darken, blur, shadow, text size and the edges are all sliders, and you see the change as you drag.

Listen and follow along

Eight reciters. The word being recited is marked as you listen, in the colour and style you choose.

Make a short

Real nature behind the words: mountains, sea, sky, night, desert, forest. Add gentle motion or a slow camera, use your own photo, or choose a free photo or video, then share.

Free, with nothing to sign up for

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Made as sadaqah jariyah.

How it works

From an ayah to a clip in a minute.

  1. Pick the ayahs

    Find a surah and tap the first and last ayah of your clip.

  2. Style it

    Choose a scene, drag the sliders, move the names where you want them. The preview is the video.

  3. Share it

    Tap Share and send the video to WhatsApp or anywhere.

Our promises

Exact Arabic. No rulings. No tracking.

The Arabic is never changed

Every Arabic word comes from one checked text, verified against a fixed checksum. The editor changes only the translation, and says so.

It shows verses, not rulings

Quran Clips points to the tafsir and to scholars. It does not interpret the Qur’an or issue fatwas.

Free, and private

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. What you read and your progress stay on your phone. Read the privacy page.
